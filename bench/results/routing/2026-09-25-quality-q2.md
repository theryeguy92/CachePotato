# Q2 — sampled quality: is the λ=0.5 model usable? (2026-09-25)

**Question:** does λ=0.5 produce usable text, or is the arm broken? Phase 6's
training-loop samples suggested λ=0.5 collapsed into token loops from step 120k
onward while λ=0 stayed coherent — which would have made the Phase D hit-rate
win worthless.

**Answer: PASS. The arm is fine, and the apparent collapse was an artifact of
the logging protocol.**

## Protocol (committed in the vault before running — Phase-7-Integration § Q2)

- 24 held-out FineWeb-Edu prompts: 48-token prefixes taken after an EOS, from
  documents longer than the prefix, so prompts are real document starts.
- 128 new tokens per prompt, **temperature 0.8 / top-p 0.95** and **greedy**.
- Both arms at step 180,000, identical corpus, fixed seed 20260925.
- Primary metric: **repetition rate R = 1 − (unique 4-grams / total 4-grams)**.
- Rule: PASS if `R(λ=0.5) ≤ max(1.5·R(λ=0), 0.15)`; FAIL if `R(λ=0.5) ≥ 0.50`
  or `≥ 3·R(λ=0)` with `R(λ=0.5) ≥ 0.20`; AMBIGUOUS otherwise.

## Results

| arm | decoding | distinct-4 | repetition R | unique-token |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| l00 (λ=0) | sampled | 0.8938 | **0.1062** | 0.5333 |
| l05 (λ=0.5) | sampled | 0.9378 | **0.0622** | 0.5702 |
| l00 (λ=0) | greedy | 0.3867 | **0.6133** | 0.2497 |
| l05 (λ=0.5) | greedy | 0.3967 | **0.6033** | 0.2575 |

**Verdict (sampled, primary): PASS.** R(λ=0.5)=0.0622 ≤ threshold 0.1593. Ratio
0.59× — λ=0.5 is *less* repetitive than the control, not more.

**Verdict (greedy, secondary): PASS.** 0.6033 vs 0.6133 — statistically
indistinguishable.

## What actually happened

The training loop logged greedy samples of only **30 tokens from a single fixed
prompt**. At 30 tokens λ=0 had not yet entered its degeneration loop; at 128
tokens it does, and just as badly as λ=0.5. So the "λ=0.5 collapses, λ=0 stays
coherent" contrast was an artifact of sample length, not a property of λ.

Sampled output from **both** arms is fluent, grammatical and on-topic. Examples
(prompt: *"...RAID 5 volume configurations... A RAID 5 volume must"*):

- λ=0 → *"be formatted with a 4-byte data type. A 4-byte RAID 5 volume should be
  formatted with a 4-byte data type. A 4-byte data type is used for operating
  systems that support a limited number of drives..."*
- λ=0.5 → *"be configured to be of a maximum performance of 1.5 Giga-hour disk
  drives. For more information on this topic, see RAID 5 Volumes and
  Requirements for RAID 5 Volumes. A RAID 5 volume must not be divided into
  multiple partitions..."*

Greedy output degrades for both (λ=0: *"RAID 5 volumes are the most common RAID
volumes. RAID 5 volumes are the most common RAID volumes..."*).

## Caveats and consequences

1. **Both arms degenerate under long greedy decoding.** That is a genuine
   (minor) quality limitation of a 184M model trained on 3B tokens, and it is
   *not* λ-specific. Recorded, not blocking — sampling is the usable mode.
2. **The training-loop sampler should not be used as a quality instrument.**
   30 greedy tokens cannot distinguish a healthy model from a looping one; it
   produced a false alarm that nearly gated Phase 7. If samples are logged in
   future runs, log ≥128 tokens with temperature.
3. **n=24 prompts, one seed.** Enough to clear the rule decisively (0.59× vs a
   1.5× threshold) but not a benchmark.
4. **Still not measured:** the real pager (Phase D used simulated LRU), tok/s at
   a RAM budget (Q3), and a second corpus shard.
